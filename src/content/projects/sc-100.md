## About the book

This Exam Ref prepares you for the SC-100 exam and helps demonstrate real-world mastery of the skills needed to design and evolve cybersecurity strategy across all aspects of enterprise architecture.

Designed for experienced IT professionals, it focuses on critical thinking and decision-making acumen needed for success at the Microsoft Certified: Cybersecurity Architect Expert level.

## Topics covered

- Zero Trust strategy and architecture
- Governance, risk, and compliance (GRC)
- Data and application security
- Security operations and identity management
- Security best practices and priorities

## Co-authors

Written with Yuri Diogenes, Mark Simos, and Gladys Rodriguez.
